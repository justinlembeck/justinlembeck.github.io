Privacy policy

Jarvis · last updated September 24, 2026

Who uses Jarvis

Jarvis is a personal assistant used only by its owner, on his own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it uses

  • Gmail (read): to summarize and search the owner's inbox when he asks.
  • Gmail (send): to send an email only after the owner hears it read back and says yes.
  • Google Calendar (read and events): to tell the owner about his schedule and add an event only after he approves it.

How it's handled

  • Sign-in tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • To answer a request, the relevant text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which processes it to produce the answer. It is not used for anything else.
  • Google data is never sold, never used for advertising, never shared with anyone else, and never used to train AI models.
  • The owner can disconnect Jarvis at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete everything Jarvis stored by deleting its folder on his computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact email on Jarvis's Google sign-in screen.
